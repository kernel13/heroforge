Exploration B — Command Rail. The derived/entered distinction becomes a place rather than a colour: everything in the dark rail is what the application worked out, and it never scrolls away; everything in the parchment column is what you typed. Reading order in the rail is what combat asks for — initiative, armour class, hit points, grapple, saves — not what the paper sheet prints. Components hide behind a disclosure so a total is never read as a sum still to be finished. Scroll: the rail stays.
